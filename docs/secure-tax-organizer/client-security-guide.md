# Keeping Your Tax Information Safe: Client Guide (Sample)

*Sample client-facing guide. The firm's name and contact details have been removed.*

Your tax documents contain your most sensitive information. Here's how our portal protects them, and the few things we ask you to do.

## What we do to protect you

- **Two-step sign-in.** Every account uses an authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator, so a stolen password alone can't open your account.
- **Private storage.** Your documents are kept in private storage. Even our staff can only open them through a link that expires in 60 seconds, and every view is recorded.
- **Need-to-know access.** Only the preparer assigned to you can see your file.
- **Less data kept.** We store only the last four digits of your Social Security number.
- **Automatic sign-out.** If you step away for 15 minutes, you're signed out.
- **Your consent first.** Before you begin, you'll be asked to consent, as federal law requires, to our use of your information to prepare your return.

## What we ask you to do

1. **Use a unique password** that you don't use anywhere else.
2. **Set up your authenticator app** when prompted, and keep your phone locked.
3. **Never send tax documents by email or text.** Upload them in the portal instead.
4. **We will never ask for your password or your 6-digit code**, by phone, email or text. If someone does, it isn't us.
5. **Sign out on shared computers**, and don't save your password on them.
6. **Tell us right away** if you get a message that looks like it's from us but feels wrong, or if you think your account was accessed.

## If you lose your phone

Contact us. After we verify your identity, an administrator can reset your two-step sign-in, and the reset is recorded in our audit log.
